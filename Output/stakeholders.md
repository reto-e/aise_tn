# Torqvoice Stakeholder List

| Name / Role | Primary Interest in the System | Influence | Impact if Needs Are Not Met |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Workshop Owner / Admin** | Full operational control: manage staff, customers, vehicles, revenue, settings, subscriptions | High | Business cannot operate; loses visibility into revenue, staff performance, and operations |
| **Technician** | Efficient work order management, inspection tracking, status updates, and time logging | Medium | Delays in service delivery; errors in diagnostics and repair records; low job satisfaction |
| **Service Advisor / Receptionist** | Quick customer check-in, quote creation, booking management, and communication with customers | High | Poor customer experience; bottlenecks in intake and handover; lost quotes |
| **Customer (Car Owner)** | Transparent service records, easy booking, clear invoices, timely communication about vehicle status | High | Customer churn; loss of trust; complaints about lack of transparency |
| **Parts Supplier** | Accurate parts orders, consistent communication channels (phone/email), reliable demand signals | Low | Stock-outs or wrong parts delivered; delays in service; cost overruns |
| **Accountant / Finance Staff** | Accurate invoices, payment tracking, tax calculations, financial reports | Medium | Compliance risk; incorrect billing; audit failures |
| **Super Admin (Platform Operator)** | System stability, multi-tenancy management, subscription plans, onboarding new workshops | High | Loss of platform revenue; inability to scale; security or compliance breaches |
| **IT / DevOps** | Reliable deployment, monitoring, data integrity, security, backup | Medium | System downtime; data loss; security incidents affecting all tenants |
| **Fleet Manager (B2B Customer)** | Centralised view of multiple vehicles, recurring service scheduling, cost tracking | Medium | Inefficient fleet maintenance; unexpected vehicle downtime; budget overruns |
| **Insurance / Warranty Provider** (external) | Verified service records, inspection reports, documented repair history | Low | Disputes over warranty claims; inability to verify repair quality |
